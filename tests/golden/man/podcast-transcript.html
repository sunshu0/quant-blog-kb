Two invented guests talk about why people stick with habits that cost them money.

Recording date: 9 January 2024

Episode Transcript

Note: this transcript was typed up for a test and has never been spoken aloud.

Dee Hostname:

Welcome back. Today we are asking why a sensible person keeps paying a fee they could easily avoid.

Guest One:

Part of it is attention. If the charge is small and arrives every month, it fades into the background like the hum of a fridge.

Part of it is also identity. People keep doing what they believe someone like them does, even when the numbers say otherwise.
That is hard to fix with a spreadsheet.

Dee Hostname:

So what would you change first, if you ran a fund?

Guest One:

I would make the default the cheap option, and I would hide the cookie jar of short-term rewards a little further back on the shelf.

Defaults do a lot of quiet work. Most people never open the settings page, so whatever is already ticked becomes the decision, and a good default can save more than a clever reminder ever will.

Dee Hostname:

Thanks for joining us on this imaginary episode.
